# Dungeon-driving asset pack

Purpose: reusable raster assets for a fantasy-dungeon car looter roguelike. Cars and salvaged weapons should feel conspicuously out of place among hand-cut stone, ancient runes, and magical loot.

## Shared visual language

- Cel-shaded, chunky silhouettes, strong charcoal ink contours, restrained angular wear.
- Cool slate dungeon surfaces; vermilion bodywork; aged brass weapons; warm bone highlights; cyan arcane energy.
- Equipment images: isolated single object, consistent elevated three-quarter view, clear silhouette, generous safe margin.
- Texture images: orthographic full-bleed material swatches. No illustrated room, perspective, or promotional mockup.
- Decals: flat overhead designs on transparent backgrounds.
- Effects: isolated single-frame particles; luminous effects can use additive blending.
- UI: angular comic-book framing with empty space for engine-rendered text.

## Deliverable boundaries

GPT Image produces raster images. Vehicle and weapon artwork is for inventory, item cards, menus, or sprites. These files are not 3D meshes, UV layouts, rigged vehicles, or animation sequences. Surface textures can be applied to the project's own 3D geometry. Individual item images are generated separately; no packed multi-item concept sheets.
